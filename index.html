A gamecast you can read

The situation is always at the top. The current at-bat shows every pitch on a strike-zone plot. Below it, completed plays scroll by in plain English, newest first, with scoring plays called out.

Everything links

Click a game for its box score, a player for his stats and game log, a team for its schedule and roster. It all comes from MLB's own live data feed.

How the gamecast works

1. The situation Score, inning, outs, count and baserunners (by name), always pinned to the top. If you look away, glance here first.
2. The at-bat The last thing that happened, in one sentence. Then the pitcher vs. batter matchup with today's lines, and every pitch: type, speed, result and where it crossed the plate.
3. The story so far Each finished plate appearance, one line apiece, grouped by half-inning with a summary when the inning ends. Click any play to see its pitches and batted-ball data.
Missed it? Any finished game can be replayed pitch by pitch, exactly as it unfolded live.

FAQ

Is it free?
Yes. There's no account and nothing to install.
Where does the data come from?
MLB's public Stats API, the same feed that powers MLB's own Gameday. Live games update every few seconds.
Is there video?
No. Diamond Cast is built for following along when you can't watch: at work, on the train, or with the game on the radio.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The layout collapses to a single column on small screens.
